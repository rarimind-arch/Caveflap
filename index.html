    How to play

    Tap, click or press space to flap. Fly through the gaps between the rocks.

    Every level takes a few more points than the last. Each one is faster and adds new hazards.

    Grab floating crystals to spend in the store. Bubbles hold power-ups: a shield, a crystal magnet, or slow motion.

    Keep your bat fed, rested, clean and happy to earn 10% more crystals.

    Rare medallions appear only in the main game. Spend them on boosts for your bat.
